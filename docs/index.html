Key Ratios
Technicals
Computed from 1Y daily closes
MACD line
12-day EMA minus 26-day EMA of the closing price. Positive means short-term momentum is running above the longer-term trend — a bullish tilt.
Signal line
A 9-day EMA of the MACD line itself. When MACD crosses above/below this line, it's read as a buy/sell trigger.
Histogram
MACD line minus signal line. Green bars above zero = bullish momentum, red bars below zero = bearish. Shrinking bars often show up before an actual crossover.
RSI (14)
A 0–100 momentum oscillator. Above 70 suggests overbought (risk of a pullback), below 30 suggests oversold (risk of a bounce).
ADX (14)
Measures how strong a trend is, 0–100, regardless of direction. Above 25 means a real trend is in place; below 20 is a choppy, range-bound market where crossovers are less reliable.
+DI / −DI
The directional indicators ADX is built from. +DI above −DI points to an uptrend; −DI above +DI points to a downtrend. Paired with ADX > 25, this gives both trend direction and strength.
